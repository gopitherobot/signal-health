What makes Signal different

The real question first
Each section leads with what you actually came to ask — how worried to be, what changes inside you, whether to keep taking something.
Honest about uncertainty
Relative descriptions, not invented numbers. Clear red flags. And a standing reminder that a website can't examine you or replace your doctor.
Built to prevent harm
Especially in medicines — the goal is to stop the dangerous, common habit of quitting a preventive treatment because you feel fine.

Educational prototype — not yet for public use. Every entry requires physician sign-off and cited clinical sources before deployment. Descriptions of frequency, risk and medicine use are illustrative teaching devices — not statistics, and not medical advice. Always follow the specific guidance you were given by your own clinician.
