● How Signal works

Honest about what we know

Every entry is stamped

Each entry carries the name of the clinician who reviewed it and the date they did. Until that happens, the entry says so on its face — no entry quietly passes itself off as checked.

No invented numbers

Frequency and risk are described in relative terms — common, uncommon, rare. We would rather say “uncommon” honestly than quote a precise-sounding figure we cannot source.

A website can't examine you

Signal explains. It does not diagnose, and it never tells you to start or stop a treatment. Every entry names the signs that mean stop reading and seek care.

Where this stands today: Signal is an educational prototype. No entry has been signed off by a named clinician yet, and every one of them says so when you open it. Nothing here is a substitute for care from your own doctor.
