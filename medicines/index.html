Medicines

Every day, or only when I need it?

Some medicines treat what you can feel. Others prevent what you cannot. Confusing the two is how people stop the tablets that were quietly keeping them well.

The single most important idea here: many of the most important medicines treat problems you cannot feel — high blood pressure, high cholesterol, a thinning of the blood. Feeling well on them means they're working. It is almost never a reason to stop.
