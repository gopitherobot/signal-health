The question nobody explains: keep taking it, or only when I have symptoms?

Most medicine confusion comes down to one thing — is this preventing something I can't feel, or treating something I can? Get that wrong and people stop life-saving tablets because they "feel fine".

The single most important idea here: many of the most important medicines treat problems you cannot feel — high blood pressure, high cholesterol, a thinning of the blood. Feeling well on them means they're working. It is almost never a reason to stop.
